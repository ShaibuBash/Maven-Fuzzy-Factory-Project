# Maven Fuzzy Factory – E-commerce Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses the e-commerce performance of Maven Fuzzy Factory using sales, website activity, marketing, product and refund data.
The objective was to evaluate business performance, understand customer engagement, identify top-performing products and marketing sources, and uncover insights that can support better business decisions.
I used Power BI, DAX and data modelling to transform the supplied datasets into an interactive, five-page dashboard. The complete interactive Power BI dashboard is available below.
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_qzYFkFNMO_FSZXWoWqzx6sLHrqKM-qm/view?usp=drive_link

**Note:** Power BI Desktop is required to open and interact with the PBIX file.

## Business Objectives
The analysis focused on answering key business questions:
•	How much revenue and gross profit did the business generate?
•	How did revenue and website sessions change over time?
•	Which products contributed most to revenue and profitability?
•	Which marketing sources generated the most website traffic and conversions?
•	How engaged were website visitors?
•	What was the scale of refunds, and which products accounted for the largest refund values?

## Tools and Technologies
•	Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization
•	DAX – Measures, KPIs and business calculations
•	Power Query – Data preparation and transformation
•	Data Modelling – Relationships between the project datasets

## Dataset
The project used the supplied Maven Fuzzy Factory datasets, including:
website_sessions, website_pageviews, orders_mav, order_items, order_item_refunds and products
These datasets provided information on website traffic, visitor activity, orders, product sales, revenue, costs and refunds.

## Dashboard Pages
## 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of overall e-commerce performance.
Key metrics:
•	Total Revenue: $1,938,510
•	Gross Profit: $1,216,140
•	Total Orders: 32,313
•	Conversion Rate: 6.83%
Visualizations include monthly revenue trends, revenue contribution by product and profitability by product.
## 2. Website Performance
Evaluates website traffic and visitor engagement.
Key metrics:
•	Total Website Sessions: 472,871
•	Total Pageviews: 1,048,575
•	Average Pages per Session: 2.22
•	Bounce Rate: 40.48%
The page also examines monthly website sessions and the most-viewed website pages.
## 3. Marketing Performance
Examines traffic sources, conversion performance and marketing campaign revenue.
Key metrics:
•	Total Marketing Sessions: 472,871
•	Total Orders: 32,313
•	Conversion Rate: 6.83%
•	Total Revenue: $1,938,510
Visualizations compare sessions and conversion rates by marketing source, alongside revenue generated by marketing campaign.
## 4. Product & Sales Performance
Evaluates product-level revenue, sales volume and profitability.
Key metrics:
•	Total Revenue: $1,938,510
•	Total Orders: 32,313
•	Total Items Sold: 40,025
•	Gross Profit: $1,216,140
The dashboard compares product revenue, gross profit contribution and monthly revenue trends.
## 5. Refund Analysis
Examines refund values, refunded items, refund rates and monthly refund trends.
Key metrics:
•	Total Refunds: $85,339
•	Net Revenue: $1,853,171
•	Refunded Items: 1,731
•	Refund Rate: 4.32%
The analysis also identifies products with the highest refund values and highlights changes in monthly refunds.

## Key Business Findings
•	The business generated approximately $1.94 million in total revenue and $1.22 million in gross profit.
•	The Original Mr. Fuzzy was the leading product by revenue and gross profit contribution.
•	The Forever Love Bear generated approximately $0.35 million in revenue, while The Birthday Sugar Panda generated approximately $0.23 million.
•	Google search (gsearch) accounted for the largest share of website sessions among the listed marketing sources.
•	The website recorded over 1 million pageviews, with an average of 2.22 pages per session.
•	Total refunds amounted to $85,339, representing a refund rate of 4.32%.
•	The Original Mr. Fuzzy accounted for the largest refund value among the products analyzed.
